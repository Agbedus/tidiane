H.E. Dr. Tidiane Ouattara is a distinguished African space leader, strategist, and international advocate for science, technology, and innovation. He is the elected President of the Council of the African Space Agency and has held senior leadership roles at the Canadian Space Agency and the African Union Commission.

About

Architect of Africa's
Space Sovereignty

H.E. Dr. Tidiane Ouattara has mobilised over US $155M for African space and Earth observation programmes, led the GMES & Africa initiative across 45 countries, and driven the ratification of the African Space Policy and Strategy by all 55 AU member states. His work bridges science and diplomacy, forging international partnerships that translate space knowledge into agricultural resilience, disaster preparedness, and climate action for African communities.

He is the elected President of the Council of the African Space Agency. A space scientist with a PhD in Remote Sensing and GIS from Université de Sherbrooke (Canada), he has built a 30+ year career at the intersection of space science, policy, and international diplomacy — holding senior leadership roles at the Canadian Space Agency, Natural Resources Canada, Environment and Climate Change Canada, and the African Union Commission.

"Space is not a luxury for Africa. It is infrastructure — as essential as roads, as vital as water."
